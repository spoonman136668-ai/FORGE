Hypothesis: a trained action-conditioned local logistic outcome predictor with inference-time history will improve previously unseen conditional-world *net motor reward at exactly equal purchased observations* over a static per-context frequency learner and time-only query selector, while retaining within 3% of an equally fully trained action-conditioned Bayesian Beta-table and a conventional fitted logistic model. If controls tie or outperform, NO native or RSI advancement. The decisive quantity is learned action-result signal, not switches by themselves.

Micro-world: 24 preaction decisions/episode. A hidden Bernoulli front hazard depends on a public context bit, previous physical action, and previous actual hazard, according to a source-secret fixed family-specific sigmoid formula. The agent chooses motor forward or left; forward returns +1 for safe and -3 for hazard; left returns 0. Agent may first purchase current hazard bit as a query, at cost 0.1. The number of queries MUST BE EXACTLY 4 per episode for every arm. Source truth is revealed to learner only through purchased hazard readings or motor forward consequence; never through hidden generative state or unpurchased left events. The environment can use previous hidden hazard to evolve; model never receives it unless publicly observed. Each arm gets own independently reset env with same seed and public context stream.

Role-splits fixed before execution: FIT 240 source-free episodes each for two known families A and B, seeds 100000..100239 and 200000..200239, policy deterministic exploration selecting query on t in {2,7,12,17} and motor forward iff (t+seed)%3!=0; all models train on the SAME recorded public action/observation/reward FIT stream. The DEV family flips signs of at least one action-conditioned predictor coefficient; not an independently authored physics source. Models and hyperparameters frozen before any DEV; no fitted-weight updates in DEV. Inference may retain public past observed signals and own actions as normal state.
