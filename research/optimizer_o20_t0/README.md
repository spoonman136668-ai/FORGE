## Falsifiable hypothesis and evaluation

An online action-conditioned local logistic predictive-state learner with fast episode-level error feedback might transfer a source-trained model of hazards to unseen world families at better net source reward per legal motor interaction when **actual observations are exactly matched**. It sees public context, its own previous motor action, previously purchased front-hazard bits, and hazard outcomes implied by its own executed forward reward. It never sees the source's private previous hazard, coefficients, or an unpurchased hazard after turning. Compare it to fully FIT-trained action/context Bayesian Beta tables (including online episode-level conditional counts), conventional logistic, context Laplace, fixed-time Beta schedule and uninformed prior; the true-source formula is a separately identified **privileged reference**, not a fair input-matched competitor.

Each method gets **four purchased one-bit hazard observations per 24-step episode** and the same public context generator and world seeds, with actions permitted to affect subsequent source hazards. All models train from **identical 8,320 public labeled FIT events** across 480 development-only episodes, then hold global weights immutable across 96 previously unfamiliar C and 96 D DEV episodes. The candidate changes only episode-local inference state from legitimately public source consequences during DEV; fitted conventional Beta also updates its episode-local belief from those same kinds of evidence. No original MiniGrid action, reset, FIT or DEV was performed.
