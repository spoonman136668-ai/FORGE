### Wingless
Study bounded two-step role-ordered affine composition learned from 4 examples of each of six frozen primitives over modulus 17. Compare exact predictions on 4 heldout cohorts (192 episodes each) with reversed-order and bag-of-roles controls. No external model or unseen label access during fitting. Primary and control criteria are in the frozen JSON.

### Yggdrasil
Study 9-slot memory under a workload shift across 12 distinct keys, using 6 non-evictable acquisition slots plus 3 free recency slots versus 9-slot LRU. The hot set of 9 keys is selected **after** initial memory acquisition, disjoint from training/cohort selection. Eight prospective heldout splits are evaluated once. All allocations remain fixed; no extra memory or oracle. The criteria are in the frozen JSON.

Both pilots are synthetic-only mechanism screens, **not** evidence of new cognitive generalization or external source transfer. Their input seeds are independent from historical S11/S12 seeds and from one another. The future executor is not allowed to load real Wingless/Yggdrasil source, private records, broker or secret credentials. Results may be SUPPORTED, MIXED, NEGATIVE or INVALID; INVALID cannot be renamed a negative. A next-study proposal may be quarantined automatically, but no second original or automatic acceptance follows from this initial sandbox.
