## The materially different target, without dispatch

The next research candidate would need to **learn an intervention-conditional predictive state** that distinguishes selected actions' attributable, *persistent and delayed* public consequences from source-environment nuisance changes. It must learn from real source selected feedback and treat unobserved unchosen outcomes as unknown, not oracle labels. A possible mechanism is a compact factorized transition belief whose state updates with observed prediction errors and whose action-conditioned multi-step forecasts are uncertainty-calibrated. Credit may be assigned to a past action only when it improves the forecast of a later **task-relevant outcome compared with an equally informed temporal and dynamics model**, not merely because that action was nearby in time.

No such novel learner is claimed implemented, trained or admitted here. Use existing separate Wingless native Go and Yggdrasil independent local-cell interfaces; preserve cold restart and source owner. A candidate must genuinely differ from (not reimplement) C34 previous-action Beta, C35 two-action count prior, C36 short-horizon turn-veto, MG23 reward trace, V12 eligibility XOR, and T37 forecast-correlation table.

**Before any new original-source use**, a legitimate source owner and independent science authority must freeze a disjoint source-family/seed roster, actions and budgets, heldout custody, exact reward/goal/transfer thresholds, negative controls and CPU/RSS/checkpoint constraints. Existing [FSA #83](https://github.com/spoonman136668-ai/FORGE/issues/83) is open and does **not** grant first-use authority. Another FORGE owner automation is not a substitute for scientific authorization.

Require at least: the unchanged native parent, fully trained same-information Bayesian/Dirichlet belief, strongest fitted recurrent or state-space predictor with matched resources, a fitted model-based planner, retained public-geometry zero-learning control, a true same-information TD/eligibility learner, exact effect-shuffled and no-memory controls. Score original physically chosen goal outcomes, future proper scoring/uncertainty, unfamiliar-composition transfer and cold-restored native action performance; compare against **strongest** legitimate control with whole-process and setup costs. Failure to beat the best fair control is a scientific **NO-GO**; insufficient observable attribution is **NOT_IDENTIFIABLE**, not assumed causal progress.

**Authority/noninterference:** This decision does not alter Wingless/Yggdrasil original source, any active training loop, FORGE F139 automation, FSA, ICE/G29 validators, protected cohorts, or accepted main. It is evidence for FORGE future model selection, not an asynchronous handoff or execution.
