## Native guard and matched original source history

The two current draft native `MG37` guards each require >=3 *prior genuine selected observations of the parent action* and >=3 of an alternative within one original public context, plus **positive alternative mean reward > parent mean +0.1**. Wingless [PR #437](https://github.com/spoonman136668-ai/Wingless/pull/437), source `mg37/reward.go` blob `8e221a3980ae5aa44a5996e71f7996ecb6432b40` and `source_bridge.go` blob `caf21f7555df34745fd1d341af7db4db7da94383`; Yggdrasil [PR #699](https://github.com/spoonman136668-ai/Yggdrasil/pull/699), source `mg37/progress_cells.py` blob `175c4a7e2ed83a37c2f103339aefc9a4e95eca86` and `source_bridge.py` blob `ed9184349dc74b43d8a28e18ac1d558512b7948c`.

The audit groups **independently by original native role AND original Farama family** (three sequential DEV seeds per role/family, no cross-role or cross-family reward pooling). It hashes the exact actor-visible *literal mission, heading/direction, front [object type, color, state]*. The positive-reward and eligibility checks are evaluated **before** incorporating each genuine selected action's own observed reward. To avoid pretending to know unobserved MG28 choices, it tests whether **any of the seven conceivable parent actions** could meet the guard with the recorded previous evidence (an optimistic support upper bound, not true native action selection).

## Scientific boundary / next stage

MG28/MG37 choices would produce different future states and rewards, which cannot be reconstructed from an off-policy history.
