## Seven preregistered models
1. **`LOCAL_LAG_PLASTICITY`**: new bounded no-gradient per-lag local votes `v_l=0,l=1..12` initialized with zero. Each true rewarded FIT action at t with observation memory `suits[t-l]` legally available updates every eligible lag by `+1` if `I(chosen_action==suit[t-l])` agrees with actual `source_reward>0`, otherwise `−1`. Choose argmax v_l (ties lower lag). Retain a **13-suit FIFO buffer during DEV** and issue source legal guess `suit[t-l*]`, default public current suit if insufficient history. Scores reflect *real source reward evidence* but rule, candidate lag basis, and read/write gating are HAND-DESIGNED, not autonomously learned cognition.
2. **`TRAINED_BAYESIAN_LAG`**: full same FIT reward actions and suits, 12 candidate lag likelihoods with fixed noise0.05, reward agreement adds log0.95, disagreement log0.05. Select max likelihood then FIFO read. This is a strong conventional matched-info method.
3. **`TRAINED_SOFT_ATTENTION`**: learned 12 trainable lag logits, `softmax` attention over eligible lagged public suits, predict probability of action success by summed attention on suit matching source action; FIT binary log-loss SGD with 6 chronological passes and fixed lr0.03; at DEV choose suit with maximum learned attention mass over lagged public history. Strong trainable conventional recurrent-buffer/attention control with actual FIT training overhead, not an externally pretrained RNN or resource-matched LLM.
4. **`TRAINED_LOGISTIC_ACTION`**: 13 trainable weights [bias, 12 binary action==past-lag observations], FIT 6 passes logistic binary reward success SGD lr0.03, L2=.001; at DEV evaluate four legal action logits and select best (ties smallest). A separately FIT-trained probabilistic conventional alternative.
5. **`SOURCE_KNOWN_LAG_ORACLE`**: privileged published-law exact source lag (k-1), no FIT and no learned latent assumption, only original legal public suit history, ceiling not a fair learning comparator.
6. **`NO_PAST_MAJORITY`**: FIT most frequent public suit (ties smallest), then always predict that suit without looking at historical stream; trained memory-erasure negative.
7. **`LOCAL_LAG_SHIFT_ABLATION`**: exact FIT-trained local lag votes from arm1 (same model/source), but shift selected lag by +1 cyclically at DEV readout; memory index causal ablation, not a separate learned candidate.

All arms see identical allowed source information during shared FIT. Original oracle knows published k and is privileged, clearly separated from properly trained Bayesian/attention/logistic.
