**Code change.** `sciencehandoff.Assess` reuses FORGE's existing `workerlease.Inspect`, `originalclaim.VerifyPredecision` and `preusebinding.FromOwnerPredecision` rather than inventing replacement authorities. It requires: one current owner ID/epoch; a held, unexpired exact-worker lease with first-use-risk FALSE; the exact prospective frozen original claim and signed owner/witness predecision; two disjoint, canonical, ordered Wingless/Yggdrasil cohort snapshots with explicit consumed/active/uncertain experiment identities; externally supplied exact content-hash pins for both; and a new D1 claim sequence strictly above the highest recorded sequence in either lane. A prospective experiment already present in either lane, or whose intent digest is reused, is rejected *even if its lease expired*. Missing or modified cohorts, stale owner, wrong source, worker, signer, policy, sequence or scope are rejected.
