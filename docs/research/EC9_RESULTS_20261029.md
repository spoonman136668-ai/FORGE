# E-C9 FIT experiment

216 local tests across 6 seeds, 3 sites, 4 conditions and 3 durations. Deterministic replay and numerical checks passed. Median benefit of construction over the cost-matched control: 0.07393 harvested resource, unchanged over durations 100, 300 and 600. No births in any run and no survivors by step 300. Consequently intergenerational benefit and learned niche construction remain unverified. Original code and data are not part of this repository record. No change to FORGE control or scientific acceptance. Next: calibrate a viable ecology before testing descendant benefits.
