## Frozen candidate and controls
- **Cells:** exactly 8 in a fixed ring with neighbor edges `(i-1)%8,(i+1)%8`. At initial public event, cell 3 stores `s1`; cell 4 stores public actual action `x1`. Two independent **trainable local retention logits** `theta3,theta4` (start `0.0`; sigmoid gate). At second public event, cell 5 receives `s2`; cell 6 the *observed public action* `x2`.
- **Transport:** two synchronous rounds of **one-hop only** packet propagation; packets contain source-authored public bit identities and gated values. Source tag at cell 3 cannot reach site 5 after one round, but can after exactly two via cell 4. Fixed message serialization is an explicit engineering scaffold, **not learned distributed representation**. Site 5 sees only signals delivered through those neighbor rounds; no direct global tuple readout.
- **Native-development readout:** one trainable logistic reward head at site 5 (eight coefficients, initial `[0.05,0.08,-0.03,0.04,-0.04,0.02,0.03,-0.02]`). Features in order `[1,g3*s1,g4*x1,s2,x2,(g3*s1)*(g4*x1),(g3*s1)*x2,(g4*x1)*x2]`. True terminal `reward=0/1` trains the head and both eligible local retention gates through a bounded **two-hop reverse credit path**; no source-private `u`, reward target, or successor leaks into the forward choice/readout. Freeze learning rates `eta=0.06` for readout and `eta_gate=0.04`, **24 chronological FIT passes**, no tuning after DEV; clamp weights to [-6,6], gate logits [-5,5].
- **Controls:** (a) full-history logistic with identical eight feature terms but no gates, same starting weights and **24 FIT passes** / LR as candidate; (b) fully FIT-trained four-bit full-history Laplace tabular and an equally informed keyed acting EMA; (c) AC-19's original context-only score remains a historical reference but is *not* an equally informed full-history control. Prior `ac18.same_information_controls` are additional references and may have fewer input bits. Fit only FIT.
- **Ablations:** independently retrain candidate with `s1=0` or `x1=0` at the candidate input, exactly same 24 FIT passes, then frozen DEV; no DEV feedback and no source reward label injection into features.

No claim of matched CPU or FLOP cost merely because both have 24 passes. True on-policy actions/returns **not measured**; source actions remain fixed.
- **Qualification:** original FIT/DEV public observations, correct bounded two-hop transport, local retention parameter change, no unauthorized input, checkpoint cold restore and frozen DEV identity; Windows/Linux GitHub-hosted source artifact replays, 0 DTR calls.
