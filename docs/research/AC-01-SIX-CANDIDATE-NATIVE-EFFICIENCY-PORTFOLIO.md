## Candidate contracts (six independent hypotheses; no presumed novel discovery)

| ID | Hypothesis / bottleneck | Primary scientific owner | Cheapest *development-only* falsifier | Strong comparable controls and killer failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AC-D1: Causal Delta Cognition** | Learned, conservative dependency selection can update affected cognitive state more cheaply than full recomputation without losing action accuracy. | Wingless (native causal state) | On development-only causal state transitions, compare delta propagation with a fully recomputed state oracle under sparse, global, hidden-dependency, and cyclic changes; count tracing overhead and false-skips. | Full recomputation, static hand-authored dependency graph, memoized update and recurrent state learner. **Reject** if unseen dependencies cause unflagged wrong decisions or full-lifecycle cost not lower. |
| **AC-D2: Decision-Sufficient Learning** | Calibrated value-of-computation/learning allocation can avoid updates that do not improve eventual decisions, without starving future transfer. | Wingless + Optimizer (advisory comparison) | Compare learned compute allocation with equal-budget exhaustive, random allocation, uncertainty-only, and myopic value-of-information policies, including delayed-value tasks. | Matched full-update learner and fully fitted decision/model-based control. **Reject** if long-horizon transfer or exploration degrades or control overhead negates savings. |
| **AC-D3: Counterexample-Driven Memory Fusion** | Merge approximately predictively equivalent histories into bounded state, and split on genuine counterexamples, retaining decision utility. | Wingless + BIO (non-executing proposal) | Force conflation traps: same short-term observations but different delayed consequences, reversed cause order, noisy observations, restart, and future disambiguating interventions. | Equal-byte episodic replay, fixed compression, tabular predictive state, learned retention controller (#238). **Reject** on irreversible silent conflations, future-query leakage, or no retained-byte benefit. |
| **AC-D4: Executable Knowledge** | Learned compact transition programs can amortize discovery cost across future interactions better than replay or repeated inference. | Wingless; relation-composition overlap with FORGE F95/F96 | Learn small programs from FIT action/outcome examples, probe out-of-domain exceptions and contradictory updates, measure search/compile/verify/storage overhead and cold reload. | F95 typed-graph reference, fitted count/Bayesian/symbolic models, fully charged program induction and cached replay. **Reject** if rules are hard-coded, use privileged labels, or amortized total cost loses. |
| **AC-D5: Delayed Causal Credit** | Locally recorded eligibility/causal traces can assign outcome credit with lower persistent state and compute than full temporal backprop or untargeted local learning. | Yggdrasil only; preserve exact native locality/message/budget/lesion constraints | Development-only delayed rewards; distractor cells, noncausal correlations, randomized outcome delays, local-message ablations and lesion/recovery, with an independently measured consequence. | Eligibility-trace/e-prop-style local update, equally trained recurrent model, random/uniform credit, acting EMA from #1031. **Reject** if action-local identifiability fails, causal attribution is ungrounded, or no generalization/cost improvement. |
| **AC-D6: Structural Transfer Compilation** | Reuse learned causal operators via bounded structural mapping between unfamiliar dynamics with little/no refitting. | Wingless and Yggdrasil independently, never shared internal state | Hold out *structurally new* families, not renamed graphs; enforce cold first-use, mismatched analogies, insufficient-information/abstention cases and mapping-search limits. | Independently fitted Bayesian/symbolic model, recurrent model, nearest-neighbor retrieval, graph-isomorphism/name-swap controls, native current mechanisms. **Reject** if success depends on family/answer metadata or mapping search outweighs adaptation savings. |

**Novelty boundary:** self-adjusting computation, metareasoning, active state learning, program induction and eligibility traces all have published precedents. Distinct novelty requires a separately demonstrated learned mechanism, actual causal ablation and reproducible advantage. Mechanism labels are not scientific claims.

## Common minimum comparison protocol (inherits ITD-02; not yet frozen)

1. **Development feasibility:** CPU-first Windows and Linux, deterministic source-visible fixtures, adversarial counterexamples, full-state/reference parity where mathematically appropriate; count instrumentation cost. Synthetic fixtures are engineering only and cannot establish new-family scientific transfer.
2. **Source gate:** Identify an **existing, project-owned, independent action→observation→measured consequence source** compatible with each candidate, with permitted API, source commit/tree/blob, observation windows, oracle separation and independently verified source birth. Current status for W1: **NOT_VERIFIED** from this AC-01 source review. Before original experiment admission, record `NO_INDEPENDENT_CONSEQUENCE_SOURCE` if legitimate verification fails; do not invent a simulator or claim T16-H satisfies W1.
3. **Matched FIT/control:** Same legitimate observations, number and order of interventions, action grammar, exposure/reward visibility and resource ceilings. Independently fit strong count/conditional/Bayesian causal/symbolic and recurrent/state-space baselines; include random and observational-only controls, counterexample/identity/retention ablations, and actual native baselines when qualified.
4. **Prospective ITD-02 W1 planning ceiling, *advisory only*:** up to 64 FIT interactions, 16 CHECK veto-only probes, 16 restart/interference probes, 32 unfamiliar-family decisions split evenly across two unfamiliar families; four predetermined seeds per arm, at least two fitted and two structurally unfamiliar families. Freeze actual source identities, budget, seeds, thresholds and witness under FORGE-owned FSA before any admitted original; CHECK may never train/select.
5. **Report all outcomes:** paired reward/regret and decision quality; post-restart usable conditional memory; calibration and information-seeking value; genuine zero-shot unfamiliar-family transfer (separate adaptation); every incorrect, abstained, inconclusive and invalid outcome; total cost including CPU/wall, peak RSS, learned/retained bytes, replay/disk/IO, messages, search, training, repair, retries, restart, qualification, and measured energy only where available.
6. **Non-negotiable:** zero hard false-positive *scientific/admission claims*; independence of source/learner/evaluator; no hidden causal graph, category, future query, answer or audit ID in candidate input; no post-hoc threshold/seed changes; no retesting used sealed originals. A simpler fully trained control matching or dominating means **NEGATIVE** for mechanistic superiority.
