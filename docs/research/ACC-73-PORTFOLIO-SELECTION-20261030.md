## Outcome and recommendation
- **U28**: exact large dense Python NTT convolution had 12–24× restricted numerical kernel advantage; sparse inputs reverse the result. Only prioritize if a real model uses this operator and full sparse-aware adaptive dispatch, model accuracy and end-to-end resources are qualified.
- **U26**: hyperbolic synthetic hierarchy retrieval improved exact-parent quality from 41/373 to 193/373, but required about 1.7–3.5× Euclidean scan cost. Its embeddings were *constructed from the target tree*, not learned. Cheap, rank-preserving distance screening may be worth a separately preregistered test if FORGE needs structured retrieval.
- **U30**: affine-support classical circuit simulator beat weak dense classical reference on limited H/X/CNOT circuits (Linux 39.83×, Windows 37.07× at 10 qubits). Useful restricted computing result; no quantum advantage and no LLM inference claim. Do not promote as an AI-performance gain.
- **U04**: true rank-4 LoRA gradient calculations matched serial correctness, but four local Python processes were slower by 1.59–5.69×. A different GPU or multi-node regime would be an independent experiment, not a retroactive win.
- **U14/U09**: exact structural reconstruction within restricted generator families; no universal exact always-shorter arbitrary-weight compression, no authentic model improvement.
- **U21/U23**: fitted 8-bit chaos and phase-blind spectral predictors lost next-character accuracy to strong fitted n-gram controls on already-exposed natural prose; do not treat these outcomes as universal disproof of recurrent native cognition or phase-preserving spectral learners.
- **U29**: topology alone cannot recover independent contradictory semantic labels; not an actual learned semantic-model experiment.
- **U03/U06**: physical capabilities **unverified**. Software interleaving and digital dot products cannot be reported as HBM or analog-in-DRAM measurements.
