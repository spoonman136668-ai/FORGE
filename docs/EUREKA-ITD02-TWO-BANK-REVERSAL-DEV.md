## Hypothesis and mechanism

The cumulative ridge numerical memory demonstrably failed after unannounced causal effect reversal. A **two-bank stable/working memory** may learn observed interventions in a working bank after independently observed contradictions and retrieve a retained stable bank when action consequences recur. No hidden regime label, source mode, future payoff, environmental action, teacher signal, world ID, or counterfactual outcome may enter the learner. Mechanism must accept only event_seq/public observation/action/actual observed consequence; it makes a read-only action proposal. It wraps the **unchanged** `research/itd02_online_consequence_memory.py` OnlineConsequenceMemory, preserving the baseline's original source/protocol and scoped authority.

Mechanism frozen: at most two action-effect memory banks, each using existing λ=0.1 per-action ridge, public dimension 2, actions 2. Train initial bank from actual observations. Once its chosen action has ≥3 supporting observations, count consecutive outcomes whose absolute prediction error is at least **0.9**. Buffer, do not overwrite stable parameters. After **two** consecutive contradictions, switch to a mature alternative bank when the alternative yields at least **0.9** less absolute residual on each buffered observation; otherwise (only if alternate bank is empty), initialize the alternate using exactly those two observed outcomes. No bank changes based on source phase boundaries. For a mature alternative, require two consecutive comparative wins; in any uncertain/third-state case abstain from claiming success rather than overwriting the retained bank. Reset contradiction buffer after a matching active observation; source-free tests audit that surprising events are neither silently converted into fabricated consequences nor disclosed as hidden labels. Record observed event count and event-chain in the wrapper, and bound to ≤10000; snapshot must include both full numerical bank snapshots, active slot, held pending observations, and verified external SHA256 plus minimum expected completed count. It must cold-restore and continue from learned banks, not replay a physical environment.
