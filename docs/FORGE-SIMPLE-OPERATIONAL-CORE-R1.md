# FORGE operational simplification — integrated research core R1

**Operational philosophy:** get one persistent FORGE controller and two independent scientific research lanes running reliably before expanding to distributed trust infrastructure. Keep scientific rigor, not needless service count.

**This increment activates NO original science.** It is the first cross-platform end-to-end **synthetic** acceptance of the composed components that already existed in FORGE. It is not a claim of deployed autonomous science.

## Architecture (target)

1. One FORGE-owned controller and transactional durable journal: exact immutable intent, one-shot start risk, verified terminal classification, bounded recovery with no blind retries.
2. Two independent Wingless and Yggdrasil work queues and isolated runners. No shared CKB-plane or KTRADE queue or lock.
3. Frozen scientific policies and portfolio with actual replayable evidence, training-only Dream-RSI, evolutionary family diversity and globally bounded budgets.
4. Independent qualified scientific admission for real original experiments, including authenticated CKB original custody and sealed-negative protections. Keep current FSA proof/checkpoint code as an **optional external adapter**, not a mandatory three-service web-of-trust in a single-host prototype.

## Critical scientific honesty

The new `fixtures/*-synthetic-history.json` files have invented, clearly synthetic epochs/source identifiers solely to exercise training-only policy selection and negative/mixed preservation. **They are NOT authenticated historical Wingless/Yggdrasil research evidence.** The pinned legacy portfolio fixtures in `cmd/research-portfolio-select` are bounded historical selection examples, not a representation of the latest scientific frontiers or an authorized next experiment.

Dream-RSI `ReplayFrozenPolicies` now executes in the **integration acceptance**, not just an open PR; it selects the training-winning replay policy. Its output remains advisory. The current frozen evolutionary portfolio is **not automatically re-ranked by Dream-RSI** after its cohort freeze (which would violate preregistration). No hidden holdout is used for policy selection.

The existing `subprocesssupervisor.Run` intentionally launches the two synthetic workers **sequentially** and stops on first ambiguity. Parallel independent real workers require a separate fully qualified scheduler implementation; do not call this concurrent science.
