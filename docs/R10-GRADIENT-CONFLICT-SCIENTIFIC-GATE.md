## New scientific method and source fidelity

W36-C2 is a frozen 16-dimensional gradient-conflict projection mechanism: for each of three source groups, calculate a train-only classification gradient; when a gradient conflicts with a different source's gradient, subtract the opposing component before averaging. This is **not** bottom-quartile weighting, learned source-invariant projection, or a modification to W36-C2 itself.

The source is pinned at draft R36/F3 commit `7f2f791f98d92ad932e0f530a7d23d0330a15643`; the exact method blob is `d75f36f25aca35e30a90bd50e31c8e0d90cedc28`, and Wingless source is `b1e4972f04142f1f35f76f9483b12491dec4846c`.

One **new** genuine development-only study uses the never-consumed 26xxx seed groups, three folds of 16 training seeds and 32 cross-development validation seeds, plus unchanged 32-epoch, 3×576 training rows and 16 parameters, compared against hard-minimax. The reserved 262xx seeds and independent 27xxx successor cohort are **never loaded**. No original scientific first use occurs.

An independent job does **not** import the method or evaluator. Instead it reconstructs every source-specific signed training example from pinned low-level Wingless code, independently repeats all 32 conflicting-gradient updates, compares the final 16 weights, recomputes every validation row, and reclassifies the frozen thresholds. Any discrepancy is a source-fidelity negative, not a reason to retune.
