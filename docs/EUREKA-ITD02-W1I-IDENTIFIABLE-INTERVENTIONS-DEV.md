## Why this is different and testable

Earlier native/Mass K4X/NR-M1X experiments found that retrospective eligibility credit may match conventional traces and attribute reward to no-effect distractor actions. Even the latest two-bank W1L mechanism did not beat switching-Bayesian adaptation on selected-action causal feedback. Here the question is specifically **identifiability of causal delay using informative actions**: can the learner choose safe or probing interventions to distinguish which previous action (t, t−1, t−2) caused the currently observed consequence, persist that hypothesis through interruption, then use the identified delay to improve multi-step decisions?

Failure mode to test explicitly: an alternating action trace has `a_t=a_{t-2}` for every `t≥2`; when observed consequence depends only on one of these action signals, lag0 and lag2 are **unidentifiable** from that trace alone. Matched independent pulse actions are necessary to distinguish them. An outcome that appears to support one lag on an unidentifiable trace must not be labeled actual causal identification.

## Frozen 7 arms, information and controls

All methods use the same 96 source-owned FIT log per world; 160 scored action choices, source consequences only for their own actions, identical public observation and cost model and causal-lag class. No arm receives source lag, schedule, future consequence or counterfactual selected action. All model-bearing arms get fully processed FIT (no deliberately undertrained controls). Specific models:
1. `pulse-causal` **candidate**: 3-state Bayesian hidden-lag belief over d∈{0,1,2}, **default hazard 0.04**, but a source-blind contradiction counter increases hazard for *the next belief update* to **0.40** after **two consecutive** actually observed outcomes whose absolute prediction residual against the model's current posterior mean is ≥ **0.70**; reset counter on noncontradictory feedback. No phase label or future truth, no retroactive belief rewrite, no parameter tuning from scored worlds. Gaussian residual sigma **0.25**. Observe: update using actual action history and actual selected outcome after subtracting known current-action cost/public nuisance. Choose action0/1 maximizing posterior-weighted *future* predicted consequence minus immediate cost, plus **0.35 ×(1−max posterior)** exploration premium for action1. A full canonical checkpoint with learned posterior, action-history, contradiction counter and digest; restore at exactly t=80 with externally checked SHA256 and floor.
2. `bayes-active`: fully trained three-state **fixed-hazard 0.04** conventional Bayesian posterior (not oracle), posterior-weighted planning, exploration premium **0.35 ×(1−max posterior)**. **This is the strongest specified standard active-Bayes control**, identical information and action budget but *without* the candidate's contradiction-triggered adaptive hazard. Implement through separately instantiated policies with no shared mutable state. The candidate is a minor adaptive-hazard variant of conventional Bayes; no general architectural novelty can be claimed even if it improves.
3. `bayes-greedy`: same Bayesian posterior but exploration premium **0** (no directed pulse bonus). This ablates information seeking with otherwise identical information, lag hypotheses and compute aside from pulse valuation.
4. `recent16`: conventional lag log-likelihood using **only** 16 latest delivered outcomes, all 3 lags and same sigma; fixed prior, chooses greedy. No source-mode oracle; recomputes solely from its own FIT/scored action history.
5. `eligibility`: conventional recency-weighted eligibility estimate of last 3 chosen action→reward correlations with weights `(1,.7,.49)`, same FIT and scored feedback, chooses greedy predicted future benefit with no privileged lag labels. If this method produces malformed predictions, classify invalid rather than silently omit.
6. `no-feedback`: heuristic action1 only when public current sine nonnegative; ignores reward, fully executes physical actions.
7. `random`: independently seeded Bernoulli 0/1, all 160 real actions, no updates.

Legitimate strongest conventional is max total observed reward across `bayes-active`, `bayes-greedy`, `recent16`, `eligibility`, selected globally across all 16 worlds. The candidate and `bayes-active` share posterior class, likelihood, public information, exploration premium, and budgets; candidate differs **only** by source-blind contradiction-triggered adaptive hazard. Distinct action traces are permitted only because their actual delivered prior feedback differs or their hazard choice diverges. Do not claim a novel learning architecture or independent scientific superiority; a same-return tie is a valid NO_GO.
