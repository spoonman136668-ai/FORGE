## New steering contract — intent versus authority

At an existing owner-controlled **new-work intake boundary** only, choose a requested `AUTO | RESEARCH | TRAIN | SELF_IMPROVE | HOLD` mode, optionally scoped to one independent lane. `HOLD` means stop admitting *new* work, not cancel/abandon existing tasks or training. User-selected modes must be authenticated, recorded with source/epoch/custody and supersedable through the same existing owner policy; no new scheduler, queue, competing consumer, chat-as-authority or new recurring task.

1. **RESEARCH:** choose independently authenticated development hypotheses, causal experiments and native integration opportunities. Absence of eligible research is an explicit idle reason, not an automatic override of manual choice.
2. **TRAIN:** choose a source-owner-verified continuation of the exact native checkpoint and frozen training protocol, preserving cumulative weights, learned state, negative history and cold restoration. Routing alone **cannot** confer a training permit, first-use, budget, protected evaluation, promotion or ref update.
3. **SELF_IMPROVE:** preserve and prospectively schedule only source-authenticated *Forge-self engineering* opportunities through the existing RSI contracts and original-owner authority, not the native-model learning lanes. Improvements can target Forge reliability, source search, task generation, research allocation, regression cost and recovery. Distinguish human-authored repairs from source generated by Forge itself. No self-modification, branch update, owner replacement, auto-merge or deployment is authorized by steering. RSI-R3's predefined two-generation ≥10%/≥10% holdout criteria and negative receipts remain in force; Eureka experiments stay separately qualified.
4. **AUTO:** rotate prospective opportunities across research, training and Forge-self source improvement when genuinely ready, using authenticated existing owner admission history and evidence of benefit; neither speculative readiness nor an exhausted finite source grammar counts as work. When two consecutive authentic *scientific* training evaluations show no gain/regression, prefer research or legitimately eligible Forge-self engineering over repeating the same training. Never derive intelligence claims from engineering CI or invented metrics. Require independent strong controls, retention, transfer and whole-process cost.
5. **HOLD:** do not admit new work, preserve and reconcile in-flight work, and continue read-only health observation. No interruption, rollback, replay or new owner process.

Manual lane selection cannot silently redirect to another lane. `FORGE-self` is a distinct, existing engineering RSI scope, **not** a fourth native-intelligence model lane; an explicit Wingless/Yggdrasil/Hybrid lane preference cannot route silently into Forge-self. Phase fairness must use only verified previous owner admissions, not untrusted model claims. Cross-lane fairness uses only genuine original-owner admissions. Unauthenticated or exhausted candidate sources are **IDLE / NEEDS SOURCE**, whereas transport or custody errors are **BLOCKED / NEEDS REPAIR**. A failed scientific experiment is a retained negative, not a retry authorization.

## Next gate: source/epoch-bound manual intent (inert)

`f139/nci04_phase_manual_intent.py` defines an exact, bounded `F139-STEER-V1 ` GitHub issue-comment shape **for future authenticated operator steering** on the already-existing F139 policy issue #807. It permits only `AUTO | RESEARCH | TRAIN | SELF_IMPROVE | HOLD`; only native research/training/auto modes may specify a native model lane. The body is tightly allowlisted with `schema`, `owner_source_sha`, `owner_epoch`, `mode`, `lane`; additional execution/first-use/merge fields are invalid.

The helper rejects a wrong issue, GitHub metadata with a non-owner identity, edited comments, malformed or duplicate-key JSON, source or owner epoch drift, and pre-consumed comment IDs. It returns only a read-only candidate and its body SHA-256, **never** an accepted control instruction, a dispatch, a scientific authorization, a stored mode, or a change to any in-flight work. The test fixture `f139/test_nci04_phase_manual_intent.py` uses synthetic metadata and exercises failure cases; it cannot prove remote author identity.

Before runtime consumption, the **existing** F139 SingleOwner must independently authenticate the GitHub response, current issue identity, original installed owner source, protected main, real owner epoch, comment order and complete replay history from its verified SQLite chain, and atomically record accepted mode transitions under the original owner lock. Do not trust a caller-supplied `consumed_comment_ids` set as proof, and do not infer AUTO from missing/unreachable comments when a persisted manual HOLD could exist. The existing owner controls all decisions; no new control plane, GitHub Actions-triggered dispatch, secret, task or policy mutation is introduced in this PR.

### Prerequisite to activating a mode

Manual intent only determines the **requested priority**. Every selected RESEARCH, TRAIN or SELF_IMPROVE candidate still requires its independently verified original lane/source credentials and eligibility. Full final exact-head Linux/Windows regression, required source admission, controlled dry-run and separate original-owner acceptance are required before merge and deployment.
