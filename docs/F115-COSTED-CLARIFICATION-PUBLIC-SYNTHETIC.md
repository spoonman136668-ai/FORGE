## Question, simulation, and public-only policy

F114 correctly refused to choose between two equally plausible source IDs when public source metadata was identical on paired hidden-target twins. F115 asks whether **one optional additional public relationship witness** would be worth acquiring, given its cost and known reliability. The witness in this experiment is **generated by the synthetic evaluator**. An "attested" source reply means it is *perfectly reliable by experimental construction*, **not** that a real outside party cryptographically authenticated it. This is a controlled value-of-information study, not a deployed evidence service or learned research improvement.

There are three untouched source-generation blocks, 48 paired synthetic examples each (24 public-identical twins per block), with all case labels and sign flips held offline:

- W115-Q, seeds 70000–70047: a source-witness service is publicly offered with declared reliability 1.0 and request cost 0.25. One successful request reveals which of the two already-public nearest source identifiers is relevant.
- W115-N, seeds 70100–70147: service declares reliability 0.5 and supplies a deterministic **uninformative** tag that is identical for both twins. Asking is not rational under the frozen utility.
- W115-H, seeds 70200–70247: a perfectly reliable additional witness is offered but costs **1.50**, exceeding its value under the declared stakes. Do not ask.

All three are identical-public-input counterfactual twin tests. Neither the offline target, case ID, seed, twin identity, nor any observation sign is passed to the selecting policy. A simulated witness response is obtainable **only after a decision to request**, at most once; after choosing, exactly one source value can be read. A public selector never reads nonselected signs.

## Locked cost and decision rule

These are **hypothetical utility units**, not estimated clinical, financial or scientific harms. Correct +1, wrong −4, abstain −0.1, request −0.25 (or −1.50 for H). Before asking, compare `p × 1 + (1 − p) × (−4) − cost` against abstaining (−0.1). Only request when strictly greater and an unresolved source tie remains.

Expected F115 constrained-policy outcome **predeclared**: Q makes 48 requests and obtains 48 correct pairs; N and H make no requests and abstain on all 48 each. Expected total utility +26.4, versus −14.4 for never-request/abstain, improvement +40.8, exceeding the frozen +20 bounded screen. These results are by design under assumed channel properties; they do **not** establish real-world reliability calibration.
