# FORGE F3 — native-generated, compiler-qualified executable learning mechanisms

## Scope and explicit limits

This is a **bounded engineering mechanism-source experiment**, not an autonomous research-authority deployment or independently accepted scientific finding. F2 remains the accepted *evaluation infrastructure* on protected main, with its preregistered scientific `DEV_NO_GO` retained unchanged.

The source-only F3 gate reuses the existing FORGE `genenv.Generate` candidate population, frozen synthetic training history and exact hashes. Three **distinct Go learning/prediction implementations** are generated by a fixed and auditable native grammar and committed in `nativediscovery/f3_generated.go`. The generator `nativediscovery/f3_source.go` reconstructs those exact bytes and the test requires byte-for-byte identity with the committed, compiled source. This validates source provenance and compiler acceptance, not an unconstrained, autonomous discovery of entirely new algorithms.

The three bounded structural mechanisms are: direct cue/mode/regime empirical table; same-cue cross-mode transfer on unobserved contexts; and same-mode cross-cue vote transfer on unobserved contexts. Their `Predict` implementations are genuinely different compiled Go functions; all receive the same `Observe` path and exact training/action budgets.

## Frozen experiment and rejection boundaries

- Baseline `c116bcab7afa3978e139a84fb4b163c1fad6520a` / accepted F2 source. F3 changes nothing in original F2, F1, F0, F139 original owner, policies, active controller, queue, Wingless or Yggdrasil repositories.
- Candidate population: exactly three frozen GenEnv vectors from original F1 training-only synthetic history, with approved ID→structural-source mapping. No arbitrary text prompt is executed, no LLM/API required, no extra scheduled process or runner.
- Two **development selection seeds 17 and 31**, with candidate selection from those only. Two **development holdout seeds 59 and 83**, used strictly after selection and with all losing candidate results preserved. **These seeds have already appeared in F2**: the holdout is NOT unseen, independently sealed scientific evidence; no `SCIENTIFIC_PASS` is permitted from these comparisons.
- Each candidate, baseline contextual table, stronger exact factorized 32-hypothesis comparator, and accepted F2 native relational learner sees the same 48 original-world action/consequence pairs, 6 changed-world probes and 16 interference transitions. At each evaluation, all arms receive the same 64 scored trials and exogenous 20% shock stream, with **no scoring feedback updates**.
- Frozen development criterion: on **both** development holdout seeds, selected new source mechanism improves by at least 5 percentage points over the best of *both conventional controls and the existing F2 native learner*, loses no more than 10 percentage points of prior-world retained accuracy and cold-restores exactly. The experiment may return `DEV_NO_GO`. `SYNTHETIC_PROVISIONAL_ONLY` is not scientific acceptance.
- Source validation and negatives: same deterministic generated source and GenEnv history digest across calls/platforms; generated source equals actual compiled `.go` bytes; correct three structural model types; invalid actions/regimes rejected, malformed snapshot and JSON trailing payload rejected, unknown generated source identities rejected. No source-writer reads runtime secrets.
- Engineering qualifications require source identity, full Python F139 regression, full Go tests, Linux race, vet, build and platform-specific retained `FORGE_NATIVE_DISCOVERY_F3` evidence.
- No claim about improved wall-clock time, GPU performance, causal reasoning, transformer replacement, RSI or authentic experimental autonomy. Native source generation is currently *constrained structural program composition*, not novel mathematical insight.

## Isolation and research continuity

Execution is *only* the existing GitHub-hosted FORGE Linux/Windows pull-request qualification. The new conditional receipt steps are limited to the F3 branch and do not execute on protected main or other research branches. The existing `AGENTS.md` isolation boundaries remain unchanged, including complete separation from CKB-plane and zero contact with Wingless/Yggdrasil owners.

No installed F139 owner, native runner, first-use science ledger, task queue, deployed binary or independently active science is accessed. Research continues in existing lanes. The F3 code is safe to merge **only as qualified optional engineering infrastructure**, and that requires exact-source platform checks plus protected-main postmerge confirmation.

## Later independent scientific gate

A genuine Eureka-like step would need candidate-generation grammar expansion, *newly* sealed unfamiliar-world samples with authentic action-consequence data, strong independently trained comparator families, equal whole-process resource budgets, multiple seeds and independent FSA qualification. Those are absent in F3 and must not be inferred from a green CI run.
