## Falsifiable new mechanism
F24 selected actions from disagreement between old/new models. F25 instead explicitly targets **identifiability of an intervention's causal effect** once a passive effect is identifiable, without an old-law archive or model disagreement. A single per-cue four-state posterior represents baseline bit and action-effect bit; only actually observed and credited outcomes update that posterior. F25 chooses action1 for a cue when passive baseline prediction p(y|do(0)) is outside [0.20,0.80], posterior effect probability P(effect=1) lies in [0.18,0.82], fewer than 3 credited action1 records exist for this cue, and no same-cue action1 intervention is still pending. Otherwise do(0). Never use hidden law, phase, future observation, uncredited outcome or scorer label in selection.

## Development worlds
Four independent synthetic DEV arms: seeds **173,179** × public cue schedules **balanced,skewed**, 96 real decisions per arm. Static, unknown-to-learner laws 173=0xb6, 179=0xd9 (four cue baseline bits plus four action-effect bits). Scorer-only truth at cue c, action a is ((law>>c)&1) XOR (a&((law>>(c+4))&1)). Balanced cue f12Mix(seed XOR uint32(t+1)*0x27d4eb2d)&3; skewed cue (t/5+seed%2)%2 except t%5==4 uses (t/5+seed%4)%4. Each selected outcome is revealed at decision t+2; erasure if f12Mix(seed XOR 0x31415926 XOR uint32(t+1)*0x9e3779b9)%13==0, else flip if f12Mix(seed XOR 0x27182818 XOR uint32(t+1)*0x85ebca6b)%17==0. At 96 decisions there are exactly 94 credits, 2 pending. All action1 interventions cost one of a 12-unit budget; do(0) is free. Do not consume first-use seeds 59/83.

## Predeclared policy portfolio and fair baselines
Eight independently acting policies: **causal-pair (candidate)**, **bayes-ig**, **bayes-error**, **thompson**, **periodic** (step%8==0), **random** (f12Mix(seed XOR uint32(t+1)*0x9e3779b9)%8==0), **no-intervention**, and **wrong-credit ablation** (candidate action policy using inverted-action evidence, scorer also using inverted-action evidence). Bayes-ig chooses do(1) iff Bernoulli predictive entropy for do(1) is greater than do(0). Bayes-error chooses do(1) iff uncertainty min(p1,1-p1) exceeds min(p0,1-p0). Thompson uses deterministic f12Mix(seed XOR uint32(t+1)*0x165667b1) to sample one posterior cue state; do(1) iff sampled effect bit=1. All tie decisions are passive. All policies have identical 4×4 uniform priors, 8:1 likelihood ratio on genuine credited outcomes, posterior capacity, observation timing, available cues, budgets and score prediction: p(y|cue,action)>0.5 predicts 1, otherwise 0. Intervened trajectories may differ; no hindsight policy selection.

## Scorer, custody, acceptance
Immediately after credit and before action at steps **24,40,56,72**, score all eight cue×action counterfactuals using scorer-only ground truth. There are 32 total points per trial, 8 per checkpoint, with 32-bit correct/incorrect trace, action SHA-256, checkpoint and terminal canonical JSON digest, intervention count, credits/pending, and approximate work counter. Cold checkpoint at step 48 (46 credited/2 pending), restore into independent state and match uninterrupted history byte-for-byte. Reject mutated/reordered credits, wrong actions, incorrect pending, unknown world/policy and noncanonical save. All comparisons are source-deterministic; no hidden labels in predictor or selector.

**F25_DEV_SIGNAL_UNVERIFIED** only if in all 4 arms causal-pair >= strongest of five conventional online controls by >=2/32, exceeds both no-intervention and wrong-credit ablations by >=3/32, spends 2..12 actions, and passes provenance/cold/negative controls. Otherwise **F25_DEV_NO_GO** (including ceiling controls). No retuning after scores. No claim of SCIENTIFIC_PASS, real-world causal intelligence, efficiency, first-use, autonomy or RSI. Strongest-control is hindsight diagnostic; controls run deployably without hindsight.
