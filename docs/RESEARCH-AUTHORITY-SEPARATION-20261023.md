# Owner-directed research authority separation — 2026-10-23

**Decision:** FORGE is the exclusive *destination* for **new** Wingless, Yggdrasil, foundational and Optimizer scientific research under its native FORGE Scientific Authority (FSA). CKB-plane remains responsible for KTRADE and for the integrity of its existing historical scientific records. Mind-Palace remains the independent shared memory/retrieval service. The operator authorizes the controlled operational transition and bounded unattended work **without recurring routine approvals**.

**This is a target state, not evidence that scientific authority has been deployed.** It does not itself grant scientific first-use, disable an existing research controller, or promote results.

## Domains and constraints

- **FORGE:** single existing F139 owner/controller and worker infrastructure for qualified native development; native FSA (once independently commissioned) exclusively admits **new FORGE-owned science**, with immutable independent source-birth, frozen preregistration, equal-information learned controls, full costs, negative retention, one-use claims, signed independent witness, and cross-host recovery. No new controller/scheduler, no paid API.
- **CKB-plane:** owns KTRADE and its original KTRADE safeguards. Original CKB-research records remain historical and are **not imported as FORGE evidence or eligibility**. CKB research wake may be retired only in a guarded, separately witnessed old-authority quiescence and cutover, with no active/uncertain scientific first-use and no overlapping new authority. Never reset, replay, or quietly alter historical receipts.
- **Mind-Palace:** remains the **sole permitted optional** cross-project context layer. FORGE retrieves private, minimal, SHA-pinned, provenance-preserving advisory context **directly from Mind-Palace**, not through CKB-plane. Mind-Palace may ingest qualified summaries by its own existing classification, provenance, source-ledger and checkpoint policies; no direct scientific promotion or raw-evidence durability is inferred from memory.
- **Wingless and Yggdrasil:** separate research architectures; existing negatives, protocols and independent first-use remain frozen. Model-improvement claims require independently reproduced unfamiliar-task transfer and matched resources, not developer smoke tests.
