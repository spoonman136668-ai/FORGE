## Distinct hypothesis

F32 tests predictive log loss using **fixed prescribed actions**. F33 instead tests **closed-loop action selection toward an external objective** when action effects arrive 1–3 steps later and overlap subsequent decisions. Hypothesis: learned multi-regime causal templates give higher budget-matched actual goal attainment than strong fully trained two-regime, pooled-event and autoregressive-history models given identical prior training interactions and **the same planning algorithm**. No oracle state/phase/law or next sensors enter the action selector. On-policy observations necessarily differ when policies choose different actions; compare identical initial states, goal targets, law schedules, noise streams, action opportunity counts and intervention caps rather than claiming identical realized trajectories.

## Frozen training and held-out worlds

Use the unchanged F32 two isolated-pulse source training worlds `f32MakeTrace(331,"isolated")` and `f32MakeTrace(337,"isolated")`, 128 actual transitions each, with 4 complete pulses/cue/source and two noisy sensors. Every model uses these **same 256 transitions**, and is cold-serialized before independent transfer worlds; no retraining or historical replay. Never infer or inject the hidden masks/lags into models.

New unseen DEV interactive seeds/schedules: 367 balanced (A→B→A→C); 373 skewed (B→A→B→D); 379 balanced (A→B→A→D); 383 skewed (B→A→B→C). Each is 96 persistent-state transitions with four contiguous 24-step hidden phases; physical action event laws are exactly F32 simulator-only `f32Law('A'/'B'/'C'/'D', cue)`. Events issued under one law retain their due time and XOR-mask after a law switch. Each world starts at hidden seed-derived 2-bit register `f27Mix(seed ^ 0x4cf5ad43)&3`. No offset. Sensors have same 90/6/4 and 80/10/10 emissions and independent F32 hash salts, using each policy's realized hidden register state. Events can overlap and XOR-cancel. Every policy sees only its own two current sensors, public cue, optional actuator action and next two sensors.

Each t=0,2,4,...,94 (48 decisions) has an externally supplied target state `goal=f27Mix(seed ^ uint32(t/2+1)*0x7f4a7c15 ^ 0x52dce729)&3`, available **before** choosing. The decision cue is `(t/2+seed%4)%4` balanced, or `(t/4+seed%2)%2` skewed except `t%8==6` uses `(t/4+seed%4)%4`. At odd t the cue remains public but action is *forced zero*, and target is scored only after the two actual transitions (t and t+1) have occurred. Each policy may issue at most **24 action1 interventions per 96-step world**; excess are prevented in policy, never silently ignored in world. Reward = number of exact target matches after two transitions, minus `0.05×number_of_action1`. Same 48 goals, 24 maximum interventions, sensor-noise hash, phase schedule, and initial hidden state for all models in that world. Record raw successes as primary metric (net reward secondary), budget usage, phase-specific successes, first two decisions after switches, and opportunity-cost accounting.

## Frozen control portfolio and action-selection rule

All neural/Bayesian controls are fully trained from identical 256 training transitions via F32 existing `f32Train` and cold-loaded before transfer:
1. **delayed-event-memory** candidate (F32 four AA/BB/AB/BA learned mask/lag templates, continually updated regime weights on actual sensor feedback).
2. **two-bank-event-bayes**, fully trained conventional A/B mixture.
3. **pooled-event-bayes**, fully trained pooled learned per-cue event law.
4. **history-logistic**, fully trained conventional 17-feature four-output multinomial softmax, four SGD passes on actual observed training and transfer sensor posterior targets. For two-step goal forecast, sum over four potential intermediate clean states and their noisy two-sensor emissions (16 sensor pairs) using only model-predicted probabilities, then predict the second step from a copied model with hypothesized observation and appended first action/cue. Do not train the copied model on synthetic feedback.
5. **history-blind**: F32 four-template candidate with all previous actual actions hidden from its two-step planner and F32 forecast/update, despite receiving the same physical action outcome sensors.
6. **cold-zero**: F32 candidate with its learned event templates replaced by fixed uniform-MAP mask1, lag1; observe real outcomes as usual.
7. **random-budget**: fixed seed/t-index random binary choices at opportunities, hard cap24; no learned model.
8. **always-zero**: never intervene; observe identical goals/environment schedule.

At each even step, for each action a∈{0,1} compute the model's **two-transition terminal probability of current public goal**, accounting for all **past actually issued events**, current hypothetical a, and forced action0 at the next odd transition. For event-template models, use each model's own learned templates and posterior mixture weights, convolve with posterior over currently observed 4-state register and XOR of due event masks at steps t and t+1; no hypothetical sensor feedback or true hidden queue is used. For history-logistic use the sensor-marginalized two-step rollout described above. Select action1 only if its predicted target probability exceeds action0 by **strictly more than 0.05**, and budget remains; otherwise choose action0. Models update only on the **actual** resulting next sensors and actions and may never observe simulator-only hidden phase, clean state or counterfactual outcomes.

All models use same action cap, goal definition, training information, noise schedule, opportunities and fixed action-decision rule (random and zero are negative controls). The number of online observed transitions is matched; differing action trajectories *legitimately* generate different next sensors. Count parameters, offline training work, planning operations, online updates and total wall-clock time; candidate may be more computationally expensive, so **do not claim matched-compute scientific qualification** merely from the common cap.

## Prespecified outcome and stop rule

Primary `F33_DEV_SIGNAL_UNVERIFIED` only if **all four** transfer worlds satisfy simultaneously: candidate obtains **≥3 more raw goal hits out of 48** than the strongest of three trained conventional planners (two-bank, pooled, logistic) scored per world; candidate net reward >= each conventional +**2.5**; candidate raw successes >= history-blind and cold-zero each +**4**; candidate uses ≤24 interventions, all policies have 96 observations and 48 opportunities, and cold/warm candidate action/observation/score/terminal SHA256 match exactly. Require deterministic entire score report, genuine effect delay (1/2/3), overlapping issued events, independent source training, no oracle or first-use, and negative-control pass. Otherwise `F33_DEV_NO_GO`. A ceiling, tie, controls that exceed candidate, incomplete CI or tampered evidence cannot be promoted. Retain all successes and failures; no post-result seed/hyperparameter/threshold retuning.

A synthetic DEV signal **never proves** independent SCIENTIFIC_PASS, RSI, general intelligence, or matched-compute efficiency; matched information/action limits do not mean matched realized observations. Scientific first-use and owner/witness execution are separately fenced.
