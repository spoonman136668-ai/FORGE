## What is actually implemented in this audit tranche

1. **Dream-RSI integrated (PR #153):** existing bounded, already-authored CKB-inspired policy replay and FORGE candidate portfolio now run together under a GitHub-hosted Linux/Windows **synthetic-only** integration. The repo-native Wingless/Yggdrasil scientific workflows were not stopped or reassigned. No real sealed evidence is consumed.
2. **Exact compositional qualification reuse guard (this branch):** `qualreuse` refuses cache reuse after *any* prereg, interface, dependency, source, harness, toolchain, cohort, budget, test-command or lane identity drift. Rejects spent first use, revealed sealed holdout, hidden budget widening and insufficient independent verification. Exactly matching authenticated focused evidence may be reused, while full regression, build and scientific admission remain mandatory. This is not an automatic runner cache or scientific authority.
3. **No unnecessary CKB FSA architecture migration:** preserve existing FSA and CKB historical evidence, but do not require three independent network trust services merely to run a non-authorizing synthetic integration.
