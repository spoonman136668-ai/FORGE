# ITD-02 research acceleration — Independent Scientific Validation

**Status:** Operator-directed, advisory steering candidate (2026-10-25). Preserves Mass Research, Adaptive Learner Comparison, L9/L10 review, independent evaluation ownership and all frozen positives/negatives.

**Outcome:** Determine whether a candidate **actually** improves native Wingless/Yggdrasil learning, retention, causal decisions, transfer or resource efficiency. Avoid spending finite research capacity maximizing weak falsifications, administrative gates or repeated tests of mechanisms already disproven.

## Validation after useful combined discovery

1. Check that the original development task is **decision-discriminating** and includes legal genuine action-selected source consequences. Treat trained-versus-untrained identical chosen actions as inconclusive for *learning benefit*, not a positive.
2. Allow qualified combined mechanisms in authorized public **development** before knowing each component's individual contribution. Do not require isolated wins before the combined exploratory attempt.
3. Freeze exact combined candidate, component/version hashes, training source and comparative hypotheses **before** attribution experiments; use targeted single-component and interaction ablations after promising exploratory outcomes.
4. Accept scientific superiority only on independently precommitted, genuinely eligible prospective evaluation using the exact native learner, equal observation/action/reward/training rights, strong trained conventional recurrent/model-based/graph/memory alternatives, cold restart and full execution/resource costs.
5. Verify unfamiliar-family transfer, causal action consequences, retained memory, interference and total CPU/RSS/storage/interaction counts. Do not equate improved prediction, action disagreement, proxy success, test count or conventional parity with native intelligence superiority.
6. Preserve negative/invalid/consumed historical evidence and stop minor retunes after decisive results. Give Core/Mechanism Discovery an actionable reason to integrate, revise the *structural hypothesis*, or stop.

Review all existing enhancement claims—Dream-RSI, Shinka/Pareto, ThetaEvolve, Promptbreeder, DGM/GenEnv, BIO, NCI-10/11/12, LCRM, continual learning, metacognition/repair and language—within their legitimately qualified scope. Use content-addressed replay, calibrated hypothesis-discrimination ranking and multi-fidelity comparisons to reduce cost without weakening decisive evidence. Candidate-generation tools never certify their own success.

Keep public development distinct from protected scientific first-use; do not reinterpret existing original-world stop contracts. FORGE/FSA authority, sealed cohorts, native model identity and Wingless/Yggdrasil independence remain unchanged. No extra controller/scheduler, API spending or automatic promotion.
