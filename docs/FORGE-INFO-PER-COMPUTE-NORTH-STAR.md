# FORGE — scientific information per compute (advisory only)

## North star

Select experiments that can **distinguish competing hypotheses**, test
**previously unfamiliar transferable capability**, or **expose a genuine
limitation**, at bounded compute cost. Do not optimize experiment-count
throughput, number of positive results, repeated benchmark successes or gate
counts. Wingless, Yggdrasil and Mass Research remain separate scientific
lanes: **do not merge their underlying evidence, hypotheses or custody**.

## ITD-02 acceleration steering (2026-10-25; advisory)

Preserve the established full optimizer portfolio, but **prioritize improved native
learner capability per real interaction and per complete unit of compute**, not
proposal, source-only test, or gate counts. The seven additive optimization
directions are: decision-discriminating authentic development environments;
a native Wingless/Yggdrasil integration fast lane; uncertainty-aware short-horizon
causal planning; reusable multistep skills; adaptive plasticity protection;
a learning-progress development curriculum; and content-addressed multi-fidelity
research allocation. These are **priorities/proposals**, not claims of deployment,
scientific advancement, or live steering authorization.

The three separately reviewable research-workspace steering supplements specify
how to use those directions without duplicating architectures or authority:

- [Core Native Intelligence](research-steering/CORE-NATIVE-INTELLIGENCE.md)
- [Learning & Mechanism Discovery](research-steering/LEARNING-MECHANISM-DISCOVERY.md)
- [Independent Scientific Validation](research-steering/INDEPENDENT-SCIENTIFIC-VALIDATION.md)

Explore compatible, qualified enhancement combinations during authorized
development **before** demanding attribution of every individual component.
Freeze exact combined identities and then perform targeted ablations and
independent prospective validation on promising results. Every candidate must
identify a real native learner consumer and an isolated, reversible integration
path. No change to hash-pinned research charters, original source roles, sealed
first-use/holdout permissions, scientific evidence, existing FORGE owner
controller, or accepted Wingless/Yggdrasil model and checkpoint state.

## Existing FORGE optimizations retained

The proposal selector recognizes existing FORGE-native candidate mechanisms:

- **Dream-RSI:** bounded recursive policy optimization from authenticated
  historical replay; independent sealed first-use remains separate.
- **Shinka/Pareto:** maintain diversity and trade-off boundaries for novel
  mechanisms versus compute.
- **ThetaEvolve:** *training-only* proposal adaptations; no training on
  apparently successful but not independently admitted observations.
- **Promptbreeder:** generate structurally different, untested proposals
  without treating a prompt mutation as progress.
- **DGM/GenEnv:** bounded structural/environment novelty; true transfer must
  be demonstrated against independently originated sources.
- **Frozen controls:** retained comparators; ensure every proposed gain is
  over the strongest same-information and resource-matched baseline.

These are **producer identities**, not authority endorsements. Native
optimization modules already exist in `dreamrsi/`, `evoportfolio/`,
`thetaadapt/`, `promptlineage/` and `genenv/`. New proposals must carry
their own source and mechanism hash and have a fully frozen pre-outcome
forecast before the advisory can rank them. No unqualified recommendation
automatically triggers a worker.

## Source-frozen calculation

`infogain.Recommend` accepts independently pinned, immutable **per-lane**
candidate forecasts. Each candidate specifies predictions over four
predeclared observable classes—support, refutation, mechanism limitation, or
uninformative—conditional on each competing hypothesis.

Expected hypothesis discrimination is the mutual information:

`I(H;O) = H(sum_h p(h) p(O|h)) - sum_h p(h) H(p(O|h))`

in forecast **microbits**. Negative and limitation observations can be highly
informative. Identical forecasts across all hypotheses provide zero
information, even when the proposed experiment is cheap or predicted to
succeed. The selector gives higher priority to this quantity than to a
forecast of transferred capability or exposed limitations. Each score is
divided by the **frozen estimate** of compute consumed, subject to an
independent portfolio compute budget, source/family diversity and a minimum
information threshold. These are preregistered predictions, **not** verified
causal benefits, measured model accuracy, scientifically accepted outcomes,
or proof of calibration.

Historical `NEGATIVE`, `MIXED`, `INVALID`,
`CONSUMED_INDETERMINATE` and `LIMITATION` records are preserved in the
content-addressed input history and carried through the recommendation.
Previously consumed source identities and mechanism hashes are excluded,
rather than rediscovered just to achieve another positive result. The
history digest prevents silent local alteration after freezing but cannot
certify that the external historical corpus is complete.

The recommendation is **never an experiment-execution grant**, a scientific
first-use attestation, an optimizer policy promotion, or an accepted research
reference. The selectors have no network, source-write, scheduler, database,
model API, broker or Cloudflare authority.

## Evidence-to-decision path (future controlled integration)

1. An independent lane source supplies authentic observation, frozen
   competing hypotheses, historical negatives, outcome protocol, fresh
   source births and actual compute accounting.
2. The optimizer freezes exact per-hypothesis predicted outcome frequencies,
   transfer expectations, cost estimates and selection weights **before**
   any candidate outcome is observed. Retain each forecast for calibration.
3. The advisory ranks proposed experiments and reports explicit
   negative-history exclusions and uncertainty. A low-information,
   non-identifiable design is rejected even if fast.
4. A *separate* qualified scientific authority decides whether any
   proposed first-use may proceed. F139's development worker and advisory
   library cannot make that decision.
5. Independent provenance and source-bound worker receipts establish
   outcomes; negative, ambiguous and failed experiments remain retained.
6. Recalibrate future *advisory* forecasts using only independently
   admitted prospective evidence, preserving sealed cohorts. Never
   retroactively tune a frozen design or use the same outcome as first
   validation of its own forecast.

## Deployment boundary

As of this research branch, FORGE's workstation F139-D2J native controller
has independently completed a finite two-worker **development-only** sequence
and preserved a 23-event ledger. This scoring package does **not** change
its scheduler or automatically enable scientific research. PR #587's issue
selector is separately draft, with no authenticated backlog or
issue-specific engineering production result. Both changes must be
independently qualified and deliberately source-transitioned before
they can change the running controller.

**Acceptance metric:** genuinely new, independently originated, valid
first-use experiments should produce calibrated information about competing
hypotheses and transfer, at fixed or lower compute, with independent
acceptance and no unsafe authorization. No synthetic pass count substitutes
for that evidence.
