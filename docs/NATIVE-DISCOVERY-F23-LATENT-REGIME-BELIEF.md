## Hypothesis

A bounded source-blind **mixture over persistent, factorized causal-law posteriors** can recognize a previously learned regime after two intervening unfamiliar regimes faster than a deployable online selector of strong conventional controls, without losing recent-regime competence. A single finite mixture state represents a *fresh* law and previously observed **unlabeled** archival law beliefs. No oracle switch, true mask, source phase, future consequence, privileged counterfactual, model/API call, new owner or background scheduler is used.

## World, observations and budget (frozen)

Four arms: `seed in {151,157}` × `policy in {balanced,skewed}`, exactly **128** action steps each. Hidden generator-only law sequence: A on steps 0–31, B on 32–63, C on 64–95, return to A on 96–127. Each law is an 8-bit mask with response `(mask>>cue)&1 XOR (action & ((mask>>(cue+4))&1))`, for 4 cues and 2 actions. Generator-only masks:

| Seed | A | B | C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 151 | `0xe2` | `0x1d` | `0x69` |
| 157 | `0x4b` | `0xd4` | `0x35` |

Exogenous, equal for every learner: balanced `cue=int(f12Mix(seed ^ uint32(t+1)*0x7f4a7c15)&3)`, `action=int(f12Mix(seed ^ uint32(t+1)*0x6a09e667)&1)`; skewed cue `(t/7+int(seed%4))%4` when `t%7==6`, otherwise `(t/4+int(seed%2))%2`, with `action=int(f12Mix(seed ^ uint32(t+1)*0xbb67ae85)&1)`. The simulator alone produces an erasure if `f12Mix(seed ^ 0x243f6a88 ^ uint32(t+1)*0x9e3779b9)%11==0`, otherwise a flip if `f12Mix(seed ^ 0x85a308d3 ^ uint32(t+1)*0x85ebca6b)%13==0`. Learners assume a fixed **8:1** correct-versus-incorrect likelihood (misspecification allowed equally for all). Exactly two-step-delayed outcomes: prior to forecasting step `t`, reveal only consequence for `t-2`; current truth is scorer-only and never fed to a learner early. At end: 126 credited, 2 pending. ## Candidate: F23 regime-belief bank, fully specified

All 256 candidate masks are represented **exactly** by four normalized 4-element cue-pair factors; uniform `.25` prior each. Fitting a credited record with value 0/1 multiplies the matching cue-pair likelihood by **8**, nonmatching by **1**, then normalizes; erasures are neutral. Prediction of *uncorrupted* response bit uses factorized posterior probability for the requested cue/action, with a tie predicted as **0**. Predictive *observed* feedback probability is `(1+7*p_match)/9` for the credited value.

At decision time, take the **last six non-erased credited** records as the evidence suffix (not six arbitrary rows), with prefix ending immediately before the earliest chosen suffix record. The fresh prior fits the last **eight credited** rows in that prefix (can include erasures; zero allowed), then consumes the suffix sequentially. The archive bank contains all completed, non-overlapping **24-credit** blocks beginning at 0 whose end lies **strictly before the evidence suffix starts**; each archive prior is the factor posterior fitted to the entire original 24 credited rows. Cap at five completed blocks (128 actions); no labels identify their generating law. No law is archived from the current evidence suffix. The recent fresh component has prior mass **0.5**, divided as **0.5** across archives; with no archives fresh gets mass **1**. For each suffix credit, compute its normalized predictive observed likelihood from each component *before* updating that component on that credit; multiply its mixture weight by that likelihood and normalize the weights, then Bayes-update its factor posterior. All updates are deterministic with ascending archive order; use float64 and reject NaN/Inf/negative probabilities. Final forecast is the mixture-weighted uncorrupted bit probability for the current action/cue, tie->0. The same ordered, credited-only computation is used on fresh and checkpoint replays. This is a finite latent-component belief, **not** a claim of optimal regime inference or exact Bayes over unknown change times.

Ablations run with equal observations: **no archive** (fresh only), **uniform retrieval** (equal component weights after updates, discarding evidence-based weights but retaining posterior updates), **wrong-credit** (feed previous action instead of actual credited action to all component fits and suffix updates), and **random retrieval** (deterministically choose one archive by `f12Mix(uint32(t)^0x93a5298d)%archive_count`, or fresh if none, with no outcome-conditioned choice). Ablation selection uses no hidden law, scorer feedback, or phase.

## Conventional controls and deployable comparator

Independently fit the original seven F22 conventional families over the full 128-step ledger, with original definitions extended only for the step bound: (1) full-history factorized Bayes; (2–4) rolling 6/8/16 credited Bayes; (5) exponential discount Bayes `0.9^age`; (6) penalized up-to-two-changepoint MLE, min 8 credited/segment, 3 correct-match penalty/switch; (7) Laplace per-cue/action empirical table, tie-to-0. Same 256-law family, 8:1 likelihood, no hidden law/phase; no learned model is reset on the unknown return.

A deployable **online selector** among those seven chooses at each forecast the control with the highest number of *credited, non-erased correct forecasted observations* in the last **12 non-erased credited** actions; tie -> listed control order, empty -> first. Its prediction for each past action must have been recorded **at that past action's decision**, prior to its delayed feedback; update the selector ledger only when the respective observed effect becomes due. Do not select retrospectively using the held-out/scorer's truth or future credit. Also report the post-hoc best-of-seven window accuracy as a clearly marked **secondary non-deployable diagnostic envelope**, never as the deployable primary comparator.
