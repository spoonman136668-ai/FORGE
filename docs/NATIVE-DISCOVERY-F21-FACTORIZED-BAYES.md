## Hypothesis

The F20 eight-bit law has four *conditionally independent* two-bit cue factors under its own uniform prior and noisy-response likelihood. Consequently, a Bayesian posterior and one-step Bayes-risk action decision can be computed exactly via four independent 4-category distributions instead of enumerating all 256 masks and repeated 256-mask conditional distributions. This is conventional exact probabilistic factorization, **not a novel cognition claim**. We expect equal decisions and predictive accuracy, but materially fewer operations and lower elapsed CPU latency. A fully optimized conventional 4-factor Bayesian solver has the *same* mathematics; efficiency gains against F20's original exhaustive implementation must not be advertised as superiority over the strongest optimized conventional algorithm or RSI.
