# NCI-04 — Optimizer pivot toward native, transferable cognition

**Date:** 2026-10-20
**Status:** operator-directed research strategy; **source-only draft**, not a deployed optimizer policy, experiment permit or scientific acceptance.
**Scope:** FORGE's existing Dream-RSI, ThetaEvolve/Theta adaptation, Pareto/evolutionary portfolio, and structural proposal search. FORGE's controller/authority code is out of scope.

## North Star

Discover **distinct non-transformer, non-token-centric cognitive mechanisms** that can support self-organizing entity representation, persistent predictive state, query-blind knowledge retention, causal composition, and cumulative learning. Wingless and Yggdrasil must remain separate architectures. Focus on mechanisms that become functional parts of their maintained native systems, not isolated synthetic benchmark score improvements.

Progress requires **independently reproduced unfamiliar-task transfer at equal or lower total compute and memory** than equally informed, equally trained strong comparators. Lower isolated Brier risk on an artificial fixture is not alone progress toward the North Star.

## Close old gates without rewriting evidence

1. Complete **already eligible** exact-head native qualification, and explicitly record blocked or unfinished checks; never declare passing results from a source-only implementation, simulated result, or partial Actions job.
2. Preserve immutable positive, negative, mixed, invalid and inconclusive evidence. Retain failed gates including T44 and exploratory T45-I–N as originally classified. Do not change frozen thresholds or retrospectively select favorable populations.
3. Stop further candidate tuning where the proposed learned mechanism matches or underperforms an equally trained recurrent, compact symbolic/count, Bayesian or nonlearned compression control. Allow **one preregistered discriminating diagnostic** only for a particular unresolved causal failure mode; keep it separate from acceptance.
4. Do not replay consumed original sources, spend prospective holdouts for development, or promote an engineering demonstration into scientific evidence.
5. Reuse qualification for unchanged exact SHA/tree and semantic inputs only; qualify bounded affected deltas, then retain one required final whole-repository/full-platform regression before acceptance/promotion.

## Family-level research allocation — not hyperparameter sweep

Each candidate must declare its mechanism family, representation, update rule, task consumer, and what native capability it enables. Prefer distinct families to mutations of an already falsified solution.

| Family | Wingless research path | Yggdrasil research path | Primary falsifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-organizing entity binding | Learn stable entities/relations from observations without named slots or teacher-provided intermediate answers | Local cells discover entity-specific functions without assigned role labels | Entity permutations, novel identities and role-removal intervention |
| Persistent predictive state | Query-blind internal state updated by prediction error and retained across task changes | Distributed state formed by local messages and maintained through perturbations | Task-switch knowledge loss, state/channel ablation |
| Learned causal composition | Learn multi-step intervention effects instead of executing supplied symbolic composition tables | Compose information transferred across separated specialist cells | Novel causal operators, unseen structural combinations, broken causal link controls |
| Continual knowledge reuse | Native working-state/snapshot and cross-task reuse on real reasoning consumers | Retain, hibernate, wake and functionally regenerate capability under fixed cell/byte budgets | Relearn-from-scratch control and post-damage function test |
| Developmental specialization | May be a baseline to Yggdrasil, not an architectural requirement | Learn local roles, differentiated update rules, and repair without central task labels | Role permutation, no-message, shared-weights and diffusion controls |

Do not require architecture convergence. A successful Wingless state updater does not prove Yggdrasil's local developmental hypothesis, and vice versa.

## How existing optimizers are redirected

- **Dream-RSI:** Improve *which distinct, source-independent cognitive hypothesis to investigate next*. Train selection only from qualified historical decision traces with genuine propensity/custody when required; reject repeat variants of resolved negative families. It cannot create eligibility or authorize dispatch.
- **ThetaEvolve / thetaadapt:** Training-only adaptation of mechanism-selection features and exploration weights. Do not use sealed evaluation or change scientific thresholds. No repeated parameter retuning of a mechanism that strong conventional controls have already falsified.
- **Pareto / evolutionary islands:** Make genuinely different representations, information-retention rules, local-cell mechanisms and causal compositional principles the diversity unit. Record predictive transfer, CPU, peak RSS, static/retained state bytes, training cost and intervention outcomes, with negative/nonfinalist retention. Do not reward duplicate hyperparameter variants as architectural diversity.
- **Promptbreeder and DGM/GenEnv:** Proposal-only paths for structurally different representation/update/consumer/test relationships; maintain the existing F94/F95 source-identity, AST and source-birth limitations. Untested novelty claims do not imply learned cognition or scientific gain.

## Minimal experiment contract before prospective first use

1. **Mechanism:** Native path and learning signal; no explicit future-query leak, teacher-supplied intermediate causal solution, conventional model hidden inside novel framing, or undisclosed additional capacity.
2. **Controls:** Equally informed, equally trained recurrent/relational competitors; strongest applicable exact symbolic/Bayesian, count, fixed local-development and diffusion controls. Include a matched-resource and a stronger unrestricted diagnostic where useful.
3. **Transfer:** Genuinely unfamiliar entity structures, operators and task sequences; separately verify operator-family independence including equivalence under permutation. Test retention and interference after task switches. Replicate independently.
4. **Causal contribution:** Remove/reorder learned state, features, messages, specialist cells or composition operators and measure the capability loss. No false hard certifications.
5. **Costs:** Complete training + inference + persistent/static decoder/storage + communication + repair costs and peak CPU/RSS/VRAM as applicable, with repeat trials. Equal/lower total resource use is required for an accepted efficiency superiority claim.
6. **Eligibility:** Frozen source generation, controls, thresholds, budgets, hashes, first-use custody and source-independent witness under FORGE-native FSA. No scientific first-use until explicitly admitted by that qualified authority.
7. **Integration:** Test an actual project-owned cognitive consumer, not only a toy fixture; keep project architecture boundaries. Qualification fast path: exact source/tree identity -> bounded patch -> cached focused tests where valid -> one final full regression -> build -> any separately authorized promotion.

If controls outperform or tie a learned candidate, record **NO-GO for claimed superiority**, preserve the negative, and switch to a different architectural family rather than increasing screens.

## Authority and non-goals

- FORGE-native FSA alone governs scientific eligibility, execution authorization, independent acceptance and promotion after its genuine operating gates have passed; this document cannot activate FSA or the FORGE controller.
- Mind-Palace supplies bounded read-only advisory context. **CKB-plane is not a FORGE execution, acceptance, witness, queue, provenance or qualification dependency** (see ADR-0002).
- No new controller, queue, scheduler, paid model API, Cloudflare mutation, live broker, token transfer, sealed-original read, production activation, acceptance or accepted-ref mutation.
- Existing Wingless/Yggdrasil T45-O candidate PRs remain draft until their **own exact-head qualification and project-native integration** are proven. A redirection of Optimizer research does not authorize their merge.

## First NCI-04 engineering boundary

Prepare **one Wingless-native** predictive-state consumer with task-cue-free retention across real project reasoning events, and **one Yggdrasil-native** locally differentiated capability with actual function recovery after targeted loss, independently. Before any new science, establish feasibility and strong controls. Reuse only exact-identity qualified foundations. Stop if a standard lookup or diffusion model does the job at lower total cost. Record outcomes, then let FORGE's existing qualified research authority decide whether any prospective experiment is eligible.
