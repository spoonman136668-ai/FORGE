## Hypothesis and world

A **source-blind archive of previously inferred causal rules**, retrieved only on contradiction with recent *credited* action consequences, recovers a returning law after a long intervening law with fewer online errors than a fully fitted portfolio of conventional Bayesian and switching models. A non-token, two-bit cue/action causal mapping is governed by an unknown 8-bit law, `((law >> cue)&1) XOR (action&((law>>(cue+4))&1))`. Only the world generator knows the law. All learners observe the identical selected action, raw cue and noisy/erased consequence with exactly **two-step-delayed credit**, no regime or phase labels, no oracle rule, future labels or counterfactual outcomes.

Four independent synthetic DEV arms: seeds **131** and **137** under two deterministic policies **balanced** and **skewed**. For seed 131, A=`0xa6` and B=`0x39`; seed 137, A=`0x5c` and B=`0xc3`. These are generator-only hidden masks. The world follows hidden A for actions **0–31**, B for **32–63**, and returns to A for **64–95** (96 actions, 94 credited, 2 pending). The action and cue trajectory is **exogenous** and identical for all models, to isolate *causal model adaptation* from action-selection effects. Balanced: `cue=int(f12Mix(seed^uint32(t+1)*0xd8163841)&3)` and `action=int(f12Mix(seed^uint32(t+1)*0x9e3779b9)&1)`. Skewed: `cue=(t/5+int(seed%4))%4` when `t%5==4`, otherwise `(t/3+int(seed%2))%2`; `action=int(f12Mix(seed^uint32(t+1)*0x85ebca6b)&1)`. Noise is predetermined by `(seed,t)` only, not action or law: outcome erased when `f12Mix(seed^0x517cc1b7^uint32(t+1)*0x9e3779b9)%7==0`, otherwise flipped when `f12Mix(seed^0xdab5a609^uint32(t+1)*0x85ebca6b)%9==0`. Learners know the *assumed* response-noise ratio **8:1** but never the generator RNG, seed or hidden law.

## Mechanisms and matched conventional controls

Every method is fitted anew at every scored time from **credited** records only and has the same eight-bit 256-law hypothesis family. The original F21 *exact* per-cue Bayesian factorization (four independent 4-entry factors) may be used as a mathematical utility, but nothing can alter F20's frozen source, tests or past scores. For any subset, multiply an initially uniform per-cue pair prior by eight for each non-erased matching credited response, one otherwise, normalizing; erased feedback is information-neutral. The scorer sees the true current uncorrupted response **only after forecasting**; it is never fed back before the due t+2 reveal. All evaluators operate only on actions `t<=93` for which the later reveal exists.

- **Candidate archive-contradiction**: fit recent **8** credited transitions. At every time at least 12 credits exist, archive the factorized rule posterior of each **completed non-overlapping 12-credit block** (blocks 0–11, 12–23, ...); archive entries are immutable and source-blind. Consider a stored block if at least three of the most recent **six credited non-erased** transitions exist. For each stored archive, score how many of these six the archive's lexicographically-first maximum-posterior rule matches, versus the rolling-8 rule on the same six. Retrieve the earliest-archived maximizer *only if* it scores at least **two more correct** than rolling-8. Otherwise use rolling-8. No hidden switch time, phase, true rule, scorer outcome or uncredited action is available.
- **Strong conventional controls, independently refitted:** complete-history Bayes; rolling-6, rolling-8 and rolling-16 Bayes; exponential-decay Bayes with per-observation likelihood log-weight factor `0.9^(age)`; penalized **one-or-two-change-point** maximum-likelihood inference over complete credited history (minimum 8 records/segment; each additional segment costs 3 correct matches, and the final segment's Bayes model predicts); and a full-history per-probe empirical table with Laplace prior, prediction majority and tie-to-zero. Choose the **highest return accuracy among all seven**, and separately earliest three-correct recovery among all seven. Every control uses the original model family and the same feedback; no hidden phase oracle or intentionally weakened baseline.
- **Mechanistic negatives:** no-archive rolling-8; unconditional best archived rule (ignores contradiction gate); and wrong-credit archive (training labels are attributed to the preceding action instead of the actual action). These ablations never replace strong controls.

Any rule chosen from a Bayesian posterior uses the highest-posterior identifiable 8-bit mask, ties by smaller integer ID. The candidate archive's rule is not given the hidden generator law. For each fitted method record deterministic work counts, but do not confuse rule operations with measured CPU or end-to-end cost.
